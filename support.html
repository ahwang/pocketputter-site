PocketPutter turns your iPhone into an overhead putting analyzer — mount it above a golf ball, putt off screen, and see your face-to-path angle, ball speed, and roll-out distance.

Requirements

  • iPhone 13 or later (the analysis needs the 240fps camera).
  • iOS 16.4 or later.

Quick setup

  1. Mount the phone 1.5–4 ft above the ground, camera pointing straight down, with the camera end of the phone toward you.
  2. Rest a golf ball on a flat surface so it sits inside the on-screen box.
  3. Putt the ball off the screen — the app captures the stroke and shows your results.

Common questions

Why do I see “Device Not Supported” on my iPhone 12 or SE?

PocketPutter measures your stroke from 240fps slow-motion video. iPhone 12, iPhone SE, and earlier models can’t record at 240fps, so the analysis can’t run. An iPhone 13 or newer is required.

Why does the screen look upside-down when I hold the phone?

The app is designed to read upright when the phone is mounted overhead with the camera end toward you. When you’re just holding it to browse or change settings, hold the phone with the charging port facing up and the screen will appear the right way around.

The app isn’t detecting my ball. What can I do?

  • Add light — even, bright lighting helps the camera find the ball.
  • Check the height — keep the phone within 1.5–4 ft of the ball so it fills enough of the frame.
  • Use a plain surface — a solid-colored mat or carpet with good contrast against a white ball works best; busy patterns and glare make detection harder.
  • Center the ball in the on-screen box and hold still until it locks on.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your phone. PocketPutter is fully offline — it collects nothing and sends nothing. See the Privacy Policy for details.
