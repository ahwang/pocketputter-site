Turn your iPhone into a putting studio.

PocketPutter reads your real putt with the camera — ball speed, face angle, and roll-out distance from a single overhead phone. No expensive sensors, no extra hardware. Just a phone stand and a golf ball.

Download on theApp Store

Requires iPhone 13 or newer (240 fps camera) · iOS 16.4+

Set up in a minute

Mount. Putt. Read the numbers.

A small tripod or a table edge is all it takes. The guided setup in the app shows you exactly how.

1

Mount your phone

1.5–4 ft above the ground, camera facing straight down, with the camera end toward you.

2

Putt off screen

Drop a ball in view, take your stroke toward a real or imagined hole, and roll it out of frame.

3

See every number

Face-to-path, ball speed, and roll-out distance appear instantly — and a physics engine calls the result.

Game modes PRO

Real putts. Virtual courses.

Your actual stroke drives the game — the ball you putt on the carpet becomes the ball on the course.

Mini golf — three championship courses

27 walled, bank-shot holes with sand, water, tides, elevation, and moving machinery. Aim with a drag, study the green from the air, and read the scorecard like a real round.

  • Saltmarsh Reach — a tidal estuary argued through ground and tide
  • The Sugarworks — a confectionery works where the floor owns your pace
  • Thunderhead Keep — a storm castle of drops, doors, and drawbridges

Skee ball — a full arcade cabinet

Nine balls up the lane, off the ramp, into the rings. A 10-foot putt is calibrated to drain the 30 exactly — chase the corner hundreds if you dare.

  • Fly-in or feed — the ball bounces, rolls back, and rides the walls to its cup, just like the real machine
  • Hi-score board — every game chases your best

Advanced stats PRO

Find your weak spot.

Strokes gained, make % by distance, weekly trends, miss patterns, speed control, and a distance-by-break heat map that points at exactly where you leak strokes. Every putt you logged before upgrading counts the moment you do — plus instant slow-motion replay.
